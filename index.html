About

Ira is an artist based in Comrie, he works in pen and ink, oils, pastels, charcoals and recently started to explore digital painting. Ira's work is inspired by the wildlife of Scotland and the world.

Studio

I am currently in the process of moving my studio from Oban to Comrie in Perthshire, check back here for updates. Email: contact@iratheobold.art
